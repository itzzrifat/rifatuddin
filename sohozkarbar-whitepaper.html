Abstract
Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) represent the operational backbone of emerging economies, driving over 25% of GDP and 80% of industrial employment in developing countries like Bangladesh. Despite their economic criticality, traditional micro and small enterprises face an acute operational dilemma: they are trapped between error-prone manual paper-based bookkeeping (Tally/Khatiyan) and overly complex, prohibitive enterprise ERP solutions engineered primarily for multinational conglomerates.

This paper introduces SohozKarbar ERP, a purpose-built, high-throughput, cloud-native enterprise resource planning system designed explicitly for emerging market trading and manufacturing merchants. We present the architectural design, algorithmic foundation of its real-time double-entry ledger synchronization engine, resilient headless automation pipeline, and lightweight database decoupling. Empirical data collected from production deployments across textile and retail distribution hubs demonstrates an 85% reduction in reconciliation discrepancies, a 4x improvement in monthly financial closing velocity, and sub-second inventory transaction guarantees across low-bandwidth edge networks.
Keywords: Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), SME Digitization, Double-Entry Accounting Engine, Distributed Ledger Sync, Micro-Architecture, SohozKarbar.

1. Introduction & Problem Formulation

In South Asian emerging markets, commerce is characterized by high transaction frequency, decentralized credit (Baki), hybrid payment modalities (cash, direct bank clearing, and mobile financial services), and variable edge connectivity. Over 70% of local merchants rely on handwritten journals or fragile standalone desktop spreadsheets. This results in severe data loss vulnerability, delayed tax and audit reporting, and unmonitored inventory shrinkage.

Commercial tier-1 ERPs (e.g., SAP, Oracle NetSuite) require capital-intensive infrastructure, lengthy implementation cycles (6–18 months), and steep end-user training curves. SohozKarbar was conceptualized under the guiding philosophy: "Simple Technology. Smarter Business." The engineering objective is to deliver enterprise-grade accounting rigor (strict GAAP/IFRS double-entry compliance) enveloped within an intuitive, low-latency interface operable on both desktop and mobile platforms.

2. System Architecture & Engineering Principles

SohozKarbar is engineered with a modular, resilient multi-tier micro-services topology optimized for resource efficiency and continuous availability:

  • Decoupled Application Tier: Backend orchestration layer built on an asynchronous, event-driven Node.js runtime, ensuring non-blocking I/O operations during peak business hours.
  • Blue-Green Deployment Protocol: To guarantee continuous operational uptime and zero-downtime hot patching, SohozKarbar enforces a strict Blue-Green deployment architecture separating Green Staging from Blue Production.
  • Headless Automation & Integration Gateway: Autonomous headless automation layer powered by isolated browser contexts and secure bot gateways (Telegram Operational Bridge) for real-time inventory alerts, daily financial health snapshots, and invoice PDF dispatch.

3. Real-Time Financial Ledger Synchronization Engine

At the heart of SohozKarbar is a deterministic, mathematically verifiable double-entry bookkeeping engine. For every economic transaction Tk committed across accounts A = {a1, a2, ... an}, the ledger enforces the fundamental invariant:

Σ Δ Debit(ai, Tk) ≡ Σ Δ Credit(ai, Tk)   &implies;   Assets = Liabilities + Equity

Transactions failing this invariant are rejected at the atomic persistence level before disk writes, guaranteeing that the general balance equation always holds.

Perpetual Inventory Valuation & COGS: SohozKarbar utilizes the Weighted Average Cost (WAC) method recalculated dynamically upon each goods received note (GRN):

Cnew = [ (Qexisting × Ccurrent) + (Qincoming × Cunit_incoming) ] / [ Qexisting + Qincoming ]
